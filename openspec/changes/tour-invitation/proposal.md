# Tour invitation

## Why
Ahiya, 3.11: "I want it to put out a notification right away for a new user, offering the tour.
The tour should be thorough so they feel the app is familiar, and also very fast, not long and
tiring." Today the tour opens by itself only right after an email or Google signup. Most new
creators now start as GUESTS (first-visit-instant-build), so they never see it at all, and the
ones who do get it without being asked.

## What
- A new creator (an account opened in the last 14 days, guest or signed up) who has not seen the
  tour gets a small invitation card: "a one minute tour?" with Start and Later.
- Later moves the invitation into the bell, as a pinned entry with the red dot, until the tour is
  completed or skipped.
- Neither ever appears for an older account or for anyone with a tour record.
- The card yields to every other guidance surface (Quick Setup, a launch confirmation, the tour)
  and to an open modal, so it never covers the creator's own work.
- The silent auto-start after signup is removed: the invitation replaces it.
