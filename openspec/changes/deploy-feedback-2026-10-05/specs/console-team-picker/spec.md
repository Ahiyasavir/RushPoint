## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Teams can be found and starred from "עכשיו"

The "הקבוצות שלי" strip on the "עכשיו" screen SHALL offer a searchable list of the run's teams, each
with a star that follows or unfollows it. The list SHALL open by itself while no team is followed,
SHALL stay open while the organizer stars teams, and its search SHALL NOT filter the Teams section.

#### Scenario: Preparing before the race
- **WHEN** the organizer opens "עכשיו" with no team followed and stars three teams
- **THEN** the list is open from the start, stays open, and three cards appear in "הקבוצות שלי"
