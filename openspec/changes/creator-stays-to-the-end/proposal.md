# Creators stay to the end of the build

## Why
Production on 30.10: most creators who have a game never ran one, and most of those stopped editing
within minutes of creating it. The new first-visit path still has four
holes: an empty dashboard between the last answer and the building moment, 16 identical text cards,
no measurement past the feed, and no ending for someone whose event is days away, because the only way
out of the walk is launching now.

## What changes
- The building moment starts on the last answer and carries through to the feed without a gap.
- The feed shows at most five highlights, then one card that lists the rest with a swap on each row.
- Funnel events run from the first answer to the launch press, plus where a person left.
- A game with a later date ends on a ready screen: days left, send to friends, add to calendar, save.
  Launching stays one press away. A game with no date is asked for one on the last screen.

## Not in this change
Email or push reminders. Event reminders today are in-app notifications only, so the ready screen
promises nothing beyond them; the calendar entry is the reminder that reaches a person who does not
come back.
