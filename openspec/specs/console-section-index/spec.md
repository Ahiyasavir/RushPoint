## Requirements
### Requirement: A console section says what it holds

A run console section with three or more panels SHALL open with an index naming each panel in the
order drawn, and choosing an entry SHALL take the organizer to that panel. The sharing section SHALL
be called "שיתוף וצוות".

#### Scenario: Opening the sharing section
- **WHEN** the organizer opens "שיתוף וצוות"
- **THEN** an index lists the join code, links and screens, staff sign-in and station QR codes, and each entry scrolls to its panel
