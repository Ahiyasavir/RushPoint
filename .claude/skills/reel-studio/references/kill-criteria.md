# Kill criteria: spot a reel that will not work BEFORE editing it

Written after reel 11 (stairs, "every hop is an expense") was shelved on 2026-10-02 after
seven edit versions: receipt in a corner, big callouts, one continuous take, rewind,
result first hook, receipt as thermal paper, text written on the steps behind him. Ahiya:
"not understood at all", "even worse than before", "if it stays like this I won't upload it".

## Why the stairs reel failed (the post mortem)

1. **The footage had no event.** A person walking down stairs, far away and small for the
   first half, at a steady pace. Nothing changes in the frame that a thumb would stop for.
   Editing can reorder and decorate an event; it cannot create one.
2. **The meaning lived in a metaphor the viewer had to decode.** "Each hop = an expense"
   only works if the viewer maps motion to money. In a scroll nobody does that mapping.
   Compare reel 10: a phone answering WhatsApp for him is understood in one frame.
3. **The payoff was weak and late.** "$40 servers", "$11 domain" do not move anyone; the
   real story (1,000 hours, nights, exams) came halfway through.
4. **No voice.** Every bit of meaning was small text over motion.
5. **Decoration grew instead of the idea.** Each round added a layer (counter, stamp,
   callouts, labels) to rescue clarity; each layer made it busier. More graphics on a weak
   shot is the signature of a concept problem.

## The pre-flight test (run on the SCRIPT and on the first ingest, before any timeline)

Score each 0 or 1. **Under 5 of 7 = do not edit it; say so and propose a reshoot or a drop.**

| # | Question | Stairs | AI reel |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the first frame show a person, a number, a stake or a mess, large in frame? | 0 (small figure on stairs) | 1 (04:04 lock screen) |
| 2 | Can a muted viewer say what the video is about after 2 seconds, with no decoding? | 0 | 1 |
| 3 | Does something visibly CHANGE every 2 to 3 seconds that IS the message (not decoration)? | 0 (same walk) | 1 (new capability, new screen) |
| 4 | Is the subject at least a third of the frame height for most of the video? | 0 (first half) | 1 |
| 5 | Is the strongest fact in the first 5 seconds? | 0 (hours came late) | 1 |
| 6 | Is there his voice, or a format that needs none (quiz, list with answers)? | 0 | 1 |
| 7 | What does the VIEWER get (something to use, learn, feel or try), not only a reason to care about him? | 0 | 1 (an assistant they can picture) |

The stairs scored 0 of 7 before the first edit. The riddles: 7. The AI reel: 7.

Question 7 was added after reel 17 (2026-10-13): "צריך להוסיף שם איזו תרומה או משהו מעניין לאנשים, למה
שהם יתנו לי לייק על זה? אין שם כלום". A script about his big day scored 6 of 6 and still gave the viewer
nothing. What fixed it was one plain sentence of what RushPoint is and "anyone can build one, free".

## Early warning signs during editing

- A second "make it clearer" round on the same footage. Clarity problems are concept
  problems until proven otherwise.
- The fix being proposed is another overlay (counter, stamp, callout, arrow).
- He says "something is missing" without being able to name it.
- Two consecutive versions where he says "worse" or "still not understood".

**Rule:** at the FIRST of these, stop editing and write him three options in plain words:
(1) drop it, (2) reshoot with a concrete shot list that fixes the failing criteria,
(3) one last edit, with the specific criterion it fixes. Recommend one. Do not silently
start version N+1.

## Script rounds (reel 17, 2026-10-13: six versions before one was accepted)

The versions that failed, in order, so the next script does not repeat them:
1. a backstory opening ("half a year ago I built an app for one race"): nothing happens in the frame;
2. a button and "I don't sleep tonight": still only about him, the viewer gets nothing;
3. a quiz with real race questions (biblical directions): "השאלות האלה לא באמת יעבדו", school, not feed;
4. three missions he demonstrates: "אל תכניס שם משימות, זה לא מעניין אנשים";
5. "you have a button like this too": not understood; the app might fail: the opposite of what he wants said.

The one he accepted was HIS structure, said in one breath (hesitate, press, phones burst onto the map,
then explain the app). **After the second rejection, ask the one question that splits the options**
("what bothers you most: the questions, the button, or that it is not you?"), and build from his answer.
