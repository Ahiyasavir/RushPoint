# Lessons from the 2026-10-01/02 session (three reels, ~25 rounds of feedback)

Three reels were built in this session: 09 riddles, 10 AI assistant (both approved,
"excellent") and 11 stairs (shelved after seven versions). Below is every mistake that
reached Ahiya, what it cost, and the process change that makes it not happen again.
Most of them are now enforced by `engine/qa.py`, not by memory.

## 0. The root mistake: not reading what already existed

The memory index listed "Reel edit rules (founder), 14 rules" and "Video-editing toolkit,
read before any video task". **I opened neither.** Then, over two days, I rediscovered
in front of him at least five things that were already written:

| Already written down | What I did instead |
|---|---|
| Rule 7: a clearly different music STYLE per video | shipped no music, then the same instruments in all three |
| Rule 1: ONE uniform speed, 1.12x | shipped 1.0x, he said "too slow", then landed on 1.12x |
| Rule 14: text above y 1420 (IG description zone) | used the brief's 1670 limit, every AI caption sits below 1420 |
| Toolkit gotcha 25: libass + Hebrew color tags | rebuilt the fix from scratch |
| Toolkit gotcha 2: mediapipe 1.0 has no `solutions` | installed mediapipe and hit it again |

**Fix:** the skill's step 0 makes reading `founder-rules.md`, this file and the toolkit
README mandatory before the first command. A brief from someone else (here: אודי, another
Claude session, relaying specs) never overrides his standing rules; where they conflict, say so.

## 1. Silent ffmpeg failures he found before QA did

| Bug | What he saw | Now enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| `amix` in the same command as the video graph stopped reading a voice input | "the recording cuts off as soon as something appears on screen" | audio mixed in its own pass + QA **voice continuity** |
| Concatenated AAC segments: priming adds up, ~0.5 s by segment 17 | "lips and voice not in sync at all", "כתב is cut" | per segment PCM audio bed + QA **lip sync** (≤ 60 ms) |
| single frame PNG + `eof_action=repeat` not held | proof cards never appeared | overlays composited inside each segment |
| `afftdn nf=-28` blind denoise | "a part of my voice just creaks" | profile learned from the silence before speech, capped |
| ffmpeg clamped an out of frame crop | head cut off at the top | `build.py` refuses a crop outside the frame |

**Fix:** a gate must measure the thing the viewer perceives (is the voice audible where it
should be, are lips in sync), not only the file format. When a NEW class of bug reaches him,
add the check to `qa.py` in the same round.

## 2. Taste mistakes (he rejected them; do not reintroduce)

- **Punch zooms and screen jumps** on graphic screens: "the zoom in looks bad, the jumps
  disturb the eye". Movement must be smooth (a slow push on a photo is fine, he asked for it
  on the violin).
- **Too many pieces and booms:** jump cuts every landing + a thump on every beat read as
  "confusing, not understandable". One continuous take beat seven cuts.
- **Icons where a real image belongs:** "don't put icons for violin and robotics, put real
  pictures". His own photos first, licensed stock second, drawn icons never.
- **Boxes over strangers:** "blur the faces in circles, not squares over the people".
- **Reading instead of hearing:** captions alone were "not pleasant to read"; his own
  narration over every line is what made reel 10 work.
- **Numbers in a corner** went unnoticed; numbers belong inside the line they count.
- **Answers:** a quiz that never shows answers felt cheap; show answers 1..n-1, keep only
  the last for the comments.
- **Timing:** early riddles short, hard riddles long. He judges time per item, not per video.

## 3. Process mistakes

- **Iterating a weak concept seven times** (stairs). See `kill-criteria.md`: the signal was
  there at version 3 and I kept polishing.
- **Re-rendering all three openings every round** (4 to 12 minutes each). Iterate on A only,
  render B/C once, at the end.
- **Trusting transcription timestamps:** faster-whisper ran ~0.5 s early. Place cuts and
  captions on energy measured speech; `scripts/ingest.py` does the shift.
- **Unpinned `pip install`:** `pip install opencv-python mediapipe` put three OpenCV packages
  on top of each other and broke `cv2`. Check the toolkit's installed list first, pin
  versions, run `python -c "import numpy, cv2"` after any install.
- **Contact sheets from `select=between(t,...)`** showed the wrong frames and sent me after a
  phantom timing bug. Extract single frames with `-ss` when the exact moment matters.
- **Peer spec churn:** reel 1 changed five times in one hour through אודי. Build the
  STRUCTURE first, fill words last; and confirm with Ahiya directly when a relayed change
  contradicts his own rules (e.g. invented percentages, "no answers").
