# Lessons from 2026-10-05 (reels 14 and 15, the missions carousel, the race opening script)

One long day: ~45 notes from Ahiya across scripts, a shoot plan PDF, two reels, a carousel and an
in app instruction video. Each note below is now a rule (`founder-rules.md` 36 to 48), a default in
code (`engine/lib/portrait.py`, `config.json`), or a `qa.py` / `check_script.py` check. The goal he
set at the end of the day: **the next reel reaches him with zero notes.** The checklist that makes
that possible is `pre-delivery-checklist.md`; run it before every send.

## 2. Editing

| What reached him | Cause | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Audio ahead of the picture | `acrossfade` at 12 joins overlapped 60 ms each: 0.7 s drift | Plain concat, frame exact segments; `qa.py` a/v drift check. |
| Every cut "very very very" visible | Same tripod framing on both sides; an 11% crop alternation did not help | Cover each join: switch to a close framing of the speaker EXACTLY at the join, an app insert, or a card (`examples/reel15-dialogue`). `qa.py` jump cut check. |
| A close up that contained the join | The crop started before the cut, so the jump happened inside it | Rule 48: the framing changes AT the join. |
| Off script words left in ("מעולה"), walk ins, head turns to the camera, the after talk | Cut at speech regions without reading them | Transcribe first, cut to the first and last SCRIPTED word, end on the last scripted line. |
| Captions too small, glued to the phone | 66 px default; phone at the top | 84 px default (`config.json`), phone big and centred (`portrait.PH_*`), captions on a plate. |
| Phone mockup small and high | `PH_TOP` 120, 1040 tall | 1240 tall, top 300. |
| Logo in every video | Old rule 12 | Rule 43: no logo; `qa.py` fails one. |
| Flat, dull colour | Phone footage untouched | `portrait.GRADE` on every take and stock clip (he loved it); `qa.py` warns under 0.20 saturation. |
| Hook not dramatic | A flat first second | Hit + whoosh at 0, music accent at 0, `zoom_in` on the hook shot, title big above the head. |
| Speech too fast in a two person talk | 1.12x everywhere | 1.12 for his monologue, about 1.03 for dialogue. |
| Mall noise, harsh voice, quiet words | Raw field audio | DeepFilterNet capped 11 dB, dynaudnorm, EQ at 3.3 and 6.5 kHz, de-esser, gentle compressor (`examples/reel15-dialogue/assemble.py`). |
| Timer not prominent, ends early | A small pill, real seconds | Rule 47: big red pill, a tick per second, 60 "seconds" spread so 0 lands on the payoff. |
| Music too quiet (twice) | gain -14 to -18 | -11; `qa.py` warns under -12.5. |
| Reshoots asked for when stock would do | Assumed he would film | Rule: offer free stock first (Mixkit, license free, `stock/LICENSES.txt`); he prefers it to reshooting. |
| QA failures sent anyway (captions 8 px into IG's zone, timer 1 px high) | QA not run before sending | **Never send without a PASS** (`pre-delivery-checklist.md` step 1). |

## 3. Process

- **Read the transcript before cutting.** Every clip of a field shoot has side talk (mic setup, English
  chatter, "now look"). The ingest transcript is the cut list.
- **Batch his notes, answer each one by number**, then rebuild once.
- **Render cost:** every PNG goes through headless Edge; `portrait.png()` now caches by HTML hash.
  Noise reduction is ~3 min; tell him before a long render.
- **Delivery:** files to him in the chat (SendUserFile). Text for WhatsApp goes to the אודי session for
  the group "תוכן" (it cannot read files on this machine). Shoot plans as a PDF (Edge print to PDF).
- **Partners change:** check `MEMORY.md` before tagging anyone (@israeli_teens ended 2026-10-05).
