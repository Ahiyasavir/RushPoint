# Lessons from 2026-10-16 (street-200: five reels from one evening of street interviews + a market booth)

He sent 17 files (one duplicated three times): 7 Samsung takes at the RushPoint Live booth in the
market, the day summary on the steps, and 8 WhatsApp copies of iPhone street takes ("200 שקל או
משימה מסתורית?"). Five reels came out of it: 18 everyone took 200 (compilation + day summary), 19 and
20 the ones who chose the mystery, 21 the 60 second pitch with a live countdown, 22 a stranger at the
booth selling the race for us. Worked example: `engine/examples/street-200/`.

## 1. "The mic was terrible" was mostly a decode, not the mic
The WhatsApp street files carry overs up to **+6.6 dB above full scale** (4 to 16% of all samples)
as a CLEAN wave, not flat tops. Any 16 bit decode (`pcm_s16le`, most players) clips every one of
them. Decode in **float with -8 dB of headroom** (`volume=-8dB`, `pcm_f32le`) and the signal is whole.
Measure before treating: `np.abs(x).max()` on a float decode, and the fraction above 1.0. A max well
above 1.0 with few "flat" runs = recoverable; then `adeclip` only adds a little.

## 2. The voice chain that keeps us sounding like ourselves (he asked for exactly that)
`audio_bench2.py` scores every candidate by DNSMOS AND by timbre drift (third octave LTAS of speech
frames vs the raw clip, level free). On four source types (street WA0009, street group WA0016, the
booth, the summary) the winner was the same: **declip -> DeepFilterNet atten 32 -> the light chain**
(high pass 80, one 1.6:1 compressor, -2 dB shelf at 5.5 kHz, limiter, loudnorm -19). OVRL 2.39->3.10,
2.25->2.77, 1.83->2.35, 2.60->2.83, voice band within ~1 dB. The 2026-10-05 field chain (dynaudnorm,
EQ cuts at 3.3/6.5 kHz, de-esser) scored about the same but moved 3 to 6 kHz by up to -4.8 dB: that IS
"doesn't sound like us". MossFormer2 at 100% wrecked SIG (2.24); Demucs alone barely helped street
noise. A slow dynaudnorm "leveller" added nothing. Drift below 150 Hz is rumble removal, not timbre.

## 3. A countdown that must land exactly ("שיפול בול על 60 שניות")
Start = the end of the word that announces it ("שניות"), stop = the end of the payoff line
("עכשיו?"), both measured by re-transcribing the ASSEMBLED take with word timestamps (Whisper on
the source is off by up to 0.5 s). The 60 timer seconds are spread over the real span (here 37.7 s).
Never slow his speech to fill it; cut dead air instead. Landed within 20 ms.

## 4. Assembly lessons (`street.py`)
- Picture segments are CACHED by a hash of everything that decides them; sound is redone every run.
  A sound only change (the chain decision) then costs seconds, not a 10 minute re-render.
- `-ss <a-2> -copyts -i src` then `trim/atrim start=a`: seeks fast and still cuts on exact source
  seconds. Without the seek, every cut decoded its source from 0 (minutes per segment on a 3 min file).
- Long cuts split automatically at caption starts (`split_long`, max 3.4 s); consecutive pieces of one
  source alternate wide/close (x1.3), aimed at the faces (`face_aim`). That satisfies rule 61 and
  rule 48 with continuous sound underneath.
- `measured.json` records every segment's part, cut index and source span, so timelines look things up
  by source second (`src_to_t`) instead of counting cuts (splitting changes the count).
- A picture can come from another moment of the same file (`va`): used when the camera was on the
  table during the last line. Pick a moment where nobody holds the mic to their mouth, or the viewer
  reads the line as theirs.
- The mediapipe landmarker finds NO faces in a 576 px street frame (short range detector). Tiles of a
  third of the width, upscaled, find them. OpenCV 5 has no haarcascades any more.
- Reels must sit at the TOP of the work folder: a nested name ("street-200/x") breaks build's file names
  and hides the music from the uniqueness check.

## 5. Captions = what was heard, not what was meant
The round trip (`check_take.py`) caught three guesses: a third boy's name ("ישעיה", unclear on tape:
dropped, asked), "אתן מפסיקות אותי" (unclear: now just "די."), and a "ו" not audible after the cut.
He corrected one more by ear: "התקבלת לצוות השיווק", not "לעצב את".

## 6. Speed
He asked twice to save time and run things in parallel. What worked: transcription in 2 workers in
priority order (street first), one JSON per file so nothing is lost; the voice bench beside the picture
renders; final masters in parallel lanes (`RP_WORKERS=2`); delivery triggered per reel as each master
lands. What cost time: a serial 1 worker ingest (13 min per 3 min file), Edge timing out at 90 s
under full CPU (retry; PNGs are cached), long heredocs with escapes (write files with the editor).
Real-ESRGAN general x4 v3 (downloaded into toolkits/bin/realesrgan/models) still takes ~15 s a frame on
this iGPU: not usable for whole videos; lanczos + hqdn3d + unsharp looked more natural anyway. The
real fix for WhatsApp footage is asking him for the originals as files.
