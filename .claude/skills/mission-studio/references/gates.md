# Gates and checks

Run after the lint (SKILL.md step 5). Hard gates first: one failure kills or forces a redesign.
Then the ten scored checks: 7 or more to ship.

## Hard gates

**G1 · Every rule is enforced.** For each rule in the text: is it enforced by the app (a time
limit, a code, a geofence, what the photo shows), by the physical setup (one key, one place), or
by the team's own interest? A rule kept only by honour, that costs the team time or points,
fails. (lessons L1)

**G2 · The laziest completion is still the mission.** Write the cheapest way to get the points.
If it skips the challenge (says the answer aloud, searches it online, photographs anything), the
mission fails. A cheaper path that needs MORE cleverness is fine; that is a Taskmaster loophole.
(L2)

**G3 · The instructions are literal.** Someone reading only the text, standing at the spot, can
take the first step without asking. No place given as "here", "nearby" or a bare distance; no
riddle or hint inside the sentence that says what to do. The puzzle may be hard, the
instructions may not. (L3)

**G4 · Strategy discovered, permissions granted.** The text does not describe the winning
strategy. Anything the winning strategy needs that a team would assume is forbidden (splitting
up, asking strangers, using a phone, leaving the route) is explicitly allowed. (L4)

**G5 · The app can verify it.** The proof is one of: photo or video that visibly shows the
result, a numeric answer, a quiz answer, a geofence arrival, a station code, sequence steps. If
"did they do it well" cannot be seen in the proof, the criterion must be in the text. Never
promise points the scoring cannot pay (variable points do not exist yet).
Photos and videos are captured LIVE in the app's own camera: no gallery, no panorama, slow motion,
reverse, filters or editing. A video is ONE clip of at most 60 s of recorded time, and it CAN be
paused and resumed (the pause does not count). A solution that needs any of the missing features is
impossible (lessons L31). Time every multi-person video's recorded seconds against one minute.

**G6 · No one is the joke.** The fun is never at the expense of a stranger who is not in on it,
and a player is never singled out to be laughed at. (Benign violation: wrong and harmless at the
same time.) Telling the stranger it is a game puts them in on it: the absurd petition does that,
and he approved it.

**G7 · Safe for minors in public.** No traffic, climbing on what is not theirs, trespassing,
buying something they must not, or being alone with an unknown adult. The text never scripts a
stranger's refusal. Do NOT write "ask permission to film" into missions: he ruled it
self-evident (lessons L17).
Splitting up is allowed only in a bounded, public area the creator chose.

**G8 · Not doable from the sofa** (field games only). If the answer can be typed without moving
or searched online, the field adds nothing.

**G10 · Not pretend play.** Delete any role or character from the text: if nothing is left to
do, the mission was make-believe, which reads as childish to 13 to 16. A role must do a job
(cover an approach, frame a real output). Same for a coordination drill with no stake and no
output worth showing (passing an object along a line): that is gym class. (L6)

**G11 · Tight clock.** A time limit is what a SHARP team really needs, not more; a hunt within
sight of the spot is 2 to 4 minutes. If unsure, halve your first number: his corrections have
halved mine every time (6 → 3, 5 → 2, 8 → 4). (L7, L32)

**G20 · Clear beats, real props, fitting scope, the funny choice.** A choreography names who
starts, the interval and the end state (L33). A scope word fits every tagged venue, or the text
gives the alternative (L34). A prop is cheap, real and named, at a named spot (L35). A flavour
(accent, genre) is the one Israeli teens already imitate (L36).

**G21 · Searchable, readable, resettable, every day.** Anything they must search for gets a search
area, never "כאן" (L37). A loophole is closed by naming the materials, never by an exception clause,
and a chain is written as a chain (L38). A mess carries its reset (L39). Nothing seasonal enters the
bank (L40). Pure silliness is tagged kids (L41). Every puzzle has its paid hint (L42).

**G22 · No double meanings, clean words, a real point, paid cooperation, shop props.** Reread key
words for a second Israeli meaning (ראשון = Sunday) (L43). Gross is fine as an example, never as a
word (L44). A camera trick, a dare menu or a counting gimmick is not a point (L45). A rival team
helps only if it gains too (L46). A prop comes from an ordinary shop (L47).

**G12 · Position-dependent text is on a located mission.** "Near you", "within N metres", "in
front of you" only on a mission with a pin, which opens on arrival; and phrased from the pin
("מהנקודה שבה אתם עומדים"). (L10)

**G13 · Hard by thought, not by missing pieces.** Every navigation clue has visible anchors;
every puzzle is solvable from what is in the field. The method of a puzzle is NOT explained in
the text (that makes it a worksheet); it can go in the paid hint. (L11, L12)

**G14 · Works in the busiest version of every tagged area.** Picture it on a crowded sidewalk,
a full mall, a school yard at break. Drop the area tag where it breaks. (L13)

**G15 · The difficulty is real and the target is measurable.** Say in one sentence what makes it
hard; if the obvious move beats it, it is not a challenge. Targets use what players carry (a
timer, their bodies, a named landmark), never an estimated distance. (L14) The target is the
TIGHTEST a good team can still hit, and when the team measures it, the unit is named so it cannot
be shrunk ("צעדים גדולים", a 1 shekel window). (L25)

**G18 · Every audience tag is earned.** Judge each tag on its own: would a group of this age
choose it over their phones? Balance, aim, catch, hide, line up, guess, spot the difference, paper
planes, passwords and spy play are kids' games: `kids`/`mixed` only. A `youth` tag needs one line
of reason (status, a real dare, a postable output, their own culture); so does `adults` or
`corporate`. Re-judge on every rewrite and every keep, never inherit. (L23)

**G19 · It has a point.** One line: the tension, the story, the result worth showing, or the
status it wins. "A nice photo" is not one. No point, delete, however clean the text. Improvising
a funny story on the spot asks for a talent; a genre frame that carries the joke does not. (L24)

**G9 · Completable for free, and money not forbidden.** No mission REQUIRES buying or spending;
every one has a free path. But do not write "בלי לקנות" or "בלי שזה יעלה כסף" either: if a team
wants to spend, that is their choice. (L5 refined)

**G16 · Every step is a field mission.** In a sequence or any multi-part mission, each step
passes the gates on its own: no sofa trivia, no mental arithmetic, nothing one person does while
the rest watch from a bench. (L20)

**G17 · Prep is specified and required.** If the mission only works when the creator prepared
something, its Quick Setup step is `required: true`, says exactly what to prepare with one concrete
example, and says what to note down for later. (L21)

## Ten scored checks (7 or more to ship)

Structure
1. Action, constraint, proof, in three sentences at most, the action first.
2. The title opens a question, three words at most.

The team
3. No one can sit out, and that is achieved by the STRUCTURE of the act (a shot each in one
   take, four places and a clock), not by an added "take turns" sentence, which he cuts (L16).
4. The team decides something.

Status and respect
5. There is a smarter way than the obvious one (a real loophole, a cleverer source, a shortcut
   that needs thought).
6. It is genuinely hard, or genuinely tense (a clock, a race). Not every team succeeds first try.

Exposure
7. The daring happens inside the team; with strangers, the player has a role that makes it
   normal.
8. Nothing requires a talent (singing, dancing, "being funny").

The experience
9. The story test: a teen tells a friend about it tomorrow. Write the sentence they would say.
10. The story test for the phone: the proof is worth posting.

## Automatic kills (no score saves these)
- A troll (G6): a stranger is the butt of a joke they are not in on. Telling them "it is part of
  a game" makes them part of it (the petition he approved, lessons L15).
- Slang, "תהיו יצירתיים", "תהיו מצחיקים", or a moral at the end.

## Never on your own authority
- **Do not delete or rewrite a mission he approved** (`reviewedCopy` or edited on the admin page),
  whatever the gates say. Propose the change; he decides. (L15)
- Drama-class warm-ups (freeze frames, pyramids, a team rap) are NOT automatic kills: he approved
  several for teens when they are done as a whole team. For a NEW mission, prefer the stronger
  categories; for an existing one, leave it.
