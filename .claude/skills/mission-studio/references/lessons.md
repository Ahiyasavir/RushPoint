## 2026-10-22 · Review of new missions 68 to 88 (the end of the review)

Approved without a note: 13 of 21. Rejected: 6. His closing: "עברתי על הכל. תסיק מסקנות לבונה,
תערוך את המשימות, תעבור על הכל ואז תכניס את המשימות הרלוונטיות לבנק".

### L43 · Read every key word for its second meaning in Israeli Hebrew
**Evidence:** הסקר אמר ("מה רוב האנשים עושים ראשון בבוקר", meant "first thing") → "תעשה שישי בבוקר,
לא ראשון, בראשון בישראל אין חופש". He read ראשון as SUNDAY. **Rule:** before shipping, reread each
key word as a reader who has never seen it: ראשון (first / Sunday), שני (second / Monday), כאן
(here / this whole area). Rephrase around the ambiguity ("הדבר הראשון שאתם עושים כשאתם קמים").
And think in the Israeli week: the weekend is Friday and Saturday.

### L44 · Funny content, clean words
**Evidence:** "קקי" in מי היה כאן → "אל תכתוב קקי באמת, תכתוב גללים". **Rule:** the gross EXAMPLE can
stay (kids love it); the WORD is the proper one. The app writes in a neat register even when it
jokes.

### L45 · A camera trick or a party game is not a point
**Evidence:** נעלמים ("חסר פואנטה לחלוטין"), טריק שוט ("תיאור גרוע וחסר פואנטה"), חיוך בחזרה ("חסר
פואנטה"), הזר מחליט ("נשמע כמו אמת או חובה, אין באמת פואנטה"). **Rule:** this is the pattern I keep
falling into after the strong ideas run out: a trick (stop motion), a dare menu, a counting gimmick.
A point is a STORY WITH THE WORLD (a stranger's answer, a find, a record someone else set) or a real
STAKE. Ask: "would a teen tell a friend about it tomorrow, in one sentence that is not 'we did a
trick'?" If not, drop it. A dwindling idea well is a reason to stop, not to lower the bar.

### L46 · Another team helps only when helping pays
**Evidence:** המעודדים (another team cheers for yours in a "first takes it" flash) → "למה שקבוצה אחרת
תעשה את זה?". **Rule:** in a race, a rival never assists for free. Cooperation between teams needs
both to gain (הפגישה: both send the same photo and both score). Check G1 from the OTHER team's chair.

### L47 · A prop must be buyable at an ordinary Israeli shop
**Evidence:** סוכריית דבש → "מאיפה ישיגו סוכריית דבש לעזאזל?". **Rule:** extends L35: if a creator
cannot pick it up at a supermarket, kiosk or stationery shop on the way, the mission does not exist
for them.

## 2026-10-22 · Review of new missions 27 to 67

Approved without a note: 26 of 41. Praised: הסוכן בקהל ("חזק מאוד!!!": find a person in a crowd by
three signs, say a password, get a code). His fixes fall into seven patterns:

### L37 · "כאן" only for what is at the pin; anything they must SEARCH for gets a search area
**Evidence:** הרצועה ("על העמוד כאן") → "אל תכתוב 'כאן' כי הם לא בטוח לידו, הם יצטרכו לחפש עמוד".
**Rule:** a located mission opens at the pin, but the object is rarely touching it. Say where to
look: "על אחד העמודים ליד [ציון דרך]", "מוסתרת מעטפה ליד [ציון דרך]". "כאן" is right only for the
spot itself.

### L38 · A clause that closes a loophole must not muddy the instruction; close it with concrete materials
**Evidence:** סירה מהחוף ("ממה שתמצאו, חוץ מבקבוק סגור") → "לא מובן מספיק הקטע עם הבקבוק הסגור ובכללי";
אותיות בטבע → "לא מובן בכלל"; מחמאה בלחישה → "לא ברור שזו אותה מחמאה שצריך ללחוש".
**Rule:** an exception bolted onto an instruction ("except a closed bottle") reads as noise. Say
what to use instead ("מענפים, עלים ופקקים"), and write a chain as a chain: "הראשון לוחש... וכל אחד
לוחש לבא את מה ששמע". Read it as a 12 year old who skims: is there one clear picture of what to do?

### L39 · A mission that makes a mess carries its reset
**Evidence:** המגדל מהמטבח → "כדאי להביא להם משימה ישר אחר כך של לסדר". **Rule:** at home especially,
the text ends with putting things back ("ובסוף הכל חוזר לארונות"), or the mission is paired with a
chore mission that follows it.

### L40 · Seasonal missions do not belong in the bank
**Evidence:** החנוכייה הכי גדולה → "לא תמיד יש חנוכיות.... מה הקטע של המשימות לחנוכה?, זה לא כל
יום". **Rule:** the bank serves every day of the year. A mission that needs a holiday, a season or
an event goes to that event's own game file, never into the bank.

### L41 · Silliness for its own sake is a kids' game (L23 extended)
**Evidence:** הנס של הרחוב → "זה לילדים, ממש לא למבוגרים"; ספרייה שקטה → "לא למבוגרים, נראלי גם לא
לנוער"; מחמאה בלחישה → "מתאים נטו לילדים". **Rule:** when the whole fun is acting silly (don't laugh,
whisper chains, "it's a miracle!" faces), tag kids. Teens and adults need a stake, a dare or an
output, not just silliness. And kids love the gross example: מי היה כאן → "או קקי".

### L42 · Every puzzle has its paid hint written
**Evidence:** חמישה חפצים → "רק שים לב שיש רמז". **Rule:** a quiz, code or numeric puzzle always ships
with `hint` + `hintPenalty`; the hint unlocks the method, never the answer.

### Also confirmed again
- **L24 (no point):** זן מוויקיפדיה ("חסר פואנטה"). Look up a fact and photograph it is homework.
- **A stranger's story beats the object:** המוצר הכי מוזר → "שיסביר למה הוא הביא אותו לחנות שלו". The
  person's reason is the content; ask for it in the text.

## 2026-10-22 · Review of new missions 1 to 26: small fixes on a third of them

**His words:** "כמו שאתה רואה התיקונים מינורים, אבל יש תיקונים, אני רוצה שתבין בדיוק אילו תיקונים אני
עושה ותשפץ את מה שעדיין לא בדקתי כי אין לי כוח לעשות תיקונים בסגנון הזה לכמעט כל משימה".
Approved without a note: 17 of 26. Praised: ארבעה דברים ("משימה נדירה!": four ordinary objects,
a 4 minute clock, splitting allowed). His fixes fall into six patterns, each now a check:

### L32 · My clocks are about twice what he wants
**Evidence:** הקבלה הארוכה 6 → "תצמצם את זה ל3 דק"; סדר ברחוב 5 → "אתה מביא זמנים ארוכים מידי, תן
להם 2 דקות"; earlier הכי רחוק בעולם 8 → 4 (L7). **Rule:** set the clock at what a SHARP team
really needs, not ×1.5; a hunt within sight of the spot is 2 to 4 minutes; when unsure, take your
number and halve it. The clock is the tension, and a team that does not finish still had fun trying.

### L33 · A sequence of who does what, over time, must name the end state
**Evidence:** הקצב של הרחוב ("כל אחד מתופף... מצטרפים אחד אחרי השני") → "לא מספיק ברור". **Rule:** when
the mission is a choreography (who starts, who joins, when), write it as countable beats: who
starts, the interval ("כל 5 שניות עוד אחד"), and what the last seconds show. Test: could two teams
read it and film the same kind of video?

### L34 · A scope word must fit every venue it is tagged for
**Evidence:** החנות הכי ותיקה ("ברחוב") → "תביא להם אפשרות לשנות את ה'רחוב'... החנות הכי ותיקה בקומה".
**Rule:** if a mission is tagged for a mall, a "street" scope breaks there. Give the alternative in
the text ("ברחוב, או בקומה אם אתם בקניון") or tag it only where the word fits.

### L35 · A prop is a real, cheap, named object, set up where the creator can actually put it
**Evidence:** השם הכי גבוה ("הדף כאן", a tall paper strip) → "מאיפה יש דף כזה גדול? שיביאו לתחנה סטיקי
נוטס... ולהדביק לקיר מסוים". **Rule:** a station prop is something a creator owns or buys at a
kiosk (sticky notes, a marker, a notebook, tape), and the setup names the exact place (a specific
wall, a pole). Never an object you would have to manufacture. Text players read names the prop
plainly ("פתק דביק"), never "the sheet here".

### L36 · Humour picks the option Israeli teens already imitate
**Evidence:** המבטא (French) → "הייתי עושה מבטא רוסי או אמריקאי, זה יותר מצחיק". **Rule:** when a mission
picks a flavour (an accent, a genre, a celebrity type), choose the one teens here already do among
themselves; the generic "international" choice is the weak one.

### Also confirmed again
- **L24 (no point = out):** ישיבה על הקיר ("אין בה פואנטה מספיק"). Endurance with a clock is not a point.
- **L23 (audience):** האות שלי ("לא מתאים למבוגרים וגם לא כל כך לנוער"). A touch-and-say word game is kids.

**Then:** missions 27 to 84 were audited against all six before he read them (see the review doc).

## 2026-10-22 · Batch 14: a mission built on a camera feature the app does not have

### L31 · Proof is captured LIVE inside the app: no gallery, no camera modes, one take
**Mission:** שמונה מכם (one of you eight times in one photo; the trick was the phone's panorama
mode). **His words:** "אבל אי אפשר להעלות תמונות מהגלריה....".
**What is true in the product:** photo proof is taken in the app's own viewfinder
(`apps/play-web/src/components/PhotoViewfinder.tsx`, getUserMedia), falling back to the phone camera
through `<input capture>`; there is no gallery picker. Video is recorded in the app as ONE clip of
at most 60 s of RECORDED time (`VIDEO_DURATION_LIMITS.ceilingSeconds`), and the recorder has a pause
button (`holdTake`/`resumeTake` in TaskRunner): a team can stop, walk, and continue the same clip,
and the time paused does not count. **His correction:** "אפשר לעצור ולהמשיך סרטון" (I had first
written "one continuous take"; read the recorder before stating a capture limit). So there is no
panorama, no slow motion, no reverse, no timelapse, no filters, no editing, no screenshot, and no
picture taken earlier; a "montage" is possible only through pause and resume inside one clip.
**Rule:** a mission's proof must be producible by pointing the app's camera at the real scene, in
one shot or one take of 60 s or less, at the moment of submission. A mission whose solution needs a
camera mode, an editor, or an old photo from the gallery is impossible (L30), however clever. A
picture the TEAM must show (an old photo, a Wikipedia page) can still appear on a SECOND phone held
in the frame. When a video packs several people or steps, time the RECORDED seconds: they must fit
in one minute, and pausing between them is allowed (worth saying in the text when the mission
needs it, since players may not know: L4). Pause also opens a format: the in-camera CUT (same pose
in six places, an object "flying" between spots, people vanishing one by one). It lives inside ONE
mission only: the recorder stops when that mission's screen closes, so a clip cannot grow across a
stage.
**Caught by:** gate G5 (extended), engine card "Proof" line.

## 2026-10-22 · Round 9: say the action, not the game

### L29 · Open with the verb; never narrate what they received or how the app works
**Mission:** ניווט לפי רמז ("קיבלתם רמז למקום, לא כתובת. תפענחו אותו כצוות ותגיעו לשם ביחד. אין
סיכה על המפה."). **His words:** "המשימה טובה, התיאור לא, פשוט תכתוב תנווטו למקום שהוא ...... בלי
להגיד קיבלתם וכל מיני שטויות, תדאג גם לעדכן את שאר הדברים על סמך זה".
**Rule:** the text is an ORDER with its content inline, not a description of the game. Never
"קיבלתם...", "התמונה שמצורפת למשימה", "מה שסומן לכם", "מחכה לכם", "אין סיכה על המפה", "כצוות":
the player can see the photo, the map and the package; telling them they have it is padding.
Write "תנווטו למקום ש[רמז]", "תלכו למקום שבתמונה", "מה השתנה כאן מאז שצולמה התמונה?", "מה יש
בחבילה העטופה?". When the content comes from the creator, the placeholder sits INSIDE that
sentence and the setup step says "replace the brackets". Applied to 15 missions in one pass.
**Caught by:** lint `meta` warning (META_NARRATION), writing.md.

**Also this round:** "טעות בתרגום" ("זה נראלי סופר מורכב") was deleted. A hunt whose target the
players may not even recognise (is this English wrong?) is too complicated, however funny the find.

## 2026-10-22 · Round 7: "it feels like you didn't progress and didn't learn anything"

**His words:** "ממש ממש התשת אותי פה, זה מרגיש שלא התקדמת ולא למדת כלום, איך חצי מהמשימות פה
פשוט על הפנים?" Eleven notes on the old missions I had kept or patched.

**Root cause:** I was SALVAGING. These were the leftovers of the bank he had already called
embarrassing, and for each one I asked "what is broken?" and patched that. Each round added a
rule (L23 to L25), and rules only catch what is broken; they never raise the bar. A weak idea with
clean wording is still a weak idea (the credits, the echo riddle). And I judged the TEXT, never
the GAME: every one of his notes comes from thirty seconds of picturing a real team playing it.

### L26 · Cute is not competitive: some missions belong only to a home game or a party
**Missions:** שתי אמיתות ושקר ("משימות חמודות אבל לא לאיזה מרוץ"), הדבר שלא ידענו ("לתייג אותה
לאירועים המתאימים רק"), the anagrams ("מתאים לתחרות בישיבה כי זו משימה דיי משעממת").
**Rule:** an icebreaker, a party game or a debrief never goes into a competitive race. Lock it:
`occasions` (a HARD filter in the composer) for an event kind, or the `home` area alone for a
home game. A seated puzzle is a seated mission: home and indoor tags only. Cute ones are for kids.

### L27 · Play it in your head before you keep it
**Missions:** הפקק ("3 בדקה זה קל מידי... 10 ב30 שניות"), מדבר בלי פה ("אף אחד בחיים לא יעלה על
זה"), האגדה המקומית ("מה זה פרט שאפשר לבדוק? מאיפה הוא יחשוב על פרט כזה?").
**Rule:** for every mission, simulate one real team, out loud, in your notes: how many seconds
the first attempt takes, whether they succeed on the first try (then it is too easy), whether
anyone can actually solve or answer it (a classic riddle nobody guesses is not hard, it is
dead), and what the stranger actually says when asked. Write the result as one line.

### L28 · The from-scratch test: would I write this today?
**Missions:** הקרדיטים ("כל כך חסרת פואנטה"), and every old mission. **Rule:** in an audit, the
question is not "what is wrong with it" but "if this did not exist, would I write it today for
this audience?". If not, delete; patching it only produces a polished weak mission. Then fill
the gap with a NEW mission built through the full workflow, which is what got 8 of 10 approved.
**Also, details:** old copy carries detail nobody needs ("צלחת, סכו״ם וכוס לכל אחד. אף אחד לא
נשכח", "הידיים בכיסים, בלי פנים", "בדיוק כמו שקיבלתם"). Cut to the act and the proof.
**Caught by:** SKILL.md bank mode step 2 (now the first question), engine card line "play it".

## 2026-10-22 · Round 6: he had a note on almost every mission I "fixed"

**His words:** "יש לי ביקורת כמעט על כל מה שכתבת אז זה אומר שלא עשית עבודה מספיק טובה, תבין למה
ותעמיק איך פעם הבאה תשתפר". Seven notes on the first seven missions I had revised in round 5.

**Root cause, honestly:** my round 5 audit was a DEFECT HUNT, not a judgment. I ran the lint and
the rule gates (honour rules, shortcuts, padding, vague words), fixed what they flagged, and called
everything else "kept". Those gates answer "is anything broken?". He reads with a different first
question: "who is this for, and would they want it?". Six of his seven notes were about that, and
my process never asked it. The ten scored checks (story test, worth posting, genuinely hard) were
in this skill the whole time; in bank mode I skipped them. Three concrete faults followed:
1. **Audience tags were inherited, not judged.** A rewrite fixed the words and kept the old tags
   ("kids, youth, adults, corporate"). A mission tagged for everyone is usually right for nobody
   over 12.
2. **"No defect" was treated as "good".** A mission can pass every gate and still have no point
   (the size illusion: correct, clear, and boring).
3. **Targets were set comfortably, not tightly.** 95 to 100 shekels, "10 steps": a target the
   team itself measures is gamed (small steps) or too easy (a 5 shekel window).

### L23 · The audience is a verdict per tag, judged on its own, every time
**Missions:** כולם או אף אחד, מרוץ המלצרים, כולם ואף אחד, כמה זה גבוה, מבחן הטיסה.
**Rule:** for every audience tag, answer separately: would a group of THIS age choose this over
their phones? Kids' games by nature, whatever the wording: balance, aim, catch, throw at a target,
hide, line up, guess a number, spot the difference, paper planes, passwords and spy play. They are
fine for kids and families and get no `youth`, `adults` or `corporate` tag. A teen tag needs a
reason in one line: status (a record, beating other teams), a real social dare with strangers, an
output worth posting, or teen culture they already have (a flip). Adults need a reason too: a real
puzzle, a real social moment, or the absurd done deadpan.
**When:** every new mission, every rewrite AND every "keep". Fixing the text does not re-judge the
audience; do it as its own step.
**Caught by:** gate G18; the review doc shows the audience on every line.

### L24 · Passing the gates is not the same as being good: every mission needs a point
**Mission:** אשליית הגודל. **His words:** "קצת משעממת וחסרת פואנטה". Nothing in it was broken.
**Rule:** before a mission is kept or written, state its point in one line: the tension, the
story they will tell, the result worth showing, or the status it wins. "A nice photo" is not a
point. No point means delete, however clean the text is. In an audit, "keep" must come with that
line written down. Also: improvising something funny or a story on the spot ("invent the
backstory") asks for a talent (scored check 8); a genre frame that does the comedy for them
(read it like a historic speech) does not.
**Caught by:** gate G19; bank mode step 2.

### L25 · A target the team measures must be tight, and worded so it cannot be gamed
**Missions:** סכום היעד ("בדיוק בין 99 ל100"), מבחן הטיסה ("10 צעדים (לא קטנים)").
**Rule:** choose the tightest target a good team can still hit; that is where the tension is. If
the team does the measuring, name the measure so the cheat is visible ("צעדים גדולים", a 1 shekel
window, a timer), never a unit they can shrink.
**Caught by:** gate G15 (extended).

## 2026-10-22 · Round 4: his second pass over the same list

### L18 · Every review document shows the CURRENT text, regenerated from the bank
**What happened:** fixes for "המפה המצוירת", "המסירה" (deleted) and "אנשים של העיר" were already in
the code, but the document he read still showed the old texts, so he reasonably concluded his notes

### L3 · The instructions are literal. Only the puzzle is allowed to be hard.
**Mission:** "ארבעה חלקים": "הקוד הוא ארבע ספרות, וכל ספרה כתובה בגיר במקום אחר, במרחק

**Rule:** two faults in one text. (a) **Vague reference**: "200 מטר מכאן" gives a distance with

(b) **A riddle inside the instructions**: "ביחד לא תספיקו" hints at the strategy obliquely, so it
reads as confusing instead of clever. Players read instructions as instructions. If there is
something to discover, put it in the content (the code, the clue), never in the sentence that
tells them what to do.
**Detect:** could someone who reads only the text, standing on the spot, take the first step

### L4 · Let them discover the strategy, and grant the permission
**Same mission.** **His fix:** "הייתי רושם 4 מקומות ואז רושם את הסדר שלהן ובכל מקום יש סיפרה
של הקוד, הם צריכים להבין שהם מחפשים קוד והם יתפצלו כדי לעשות את זה מהר יותר. גם צריך להביא
להם אישור להתפצל".
**Rule:** do not tell them the smart strategy, and do not hint at it. Build a structure where the
smart strategy pays off (four places plus a clock), state the facts plainly, and let them find it.
That discovery is the "aha", and it belongs to them. **But** if the smart strategy is something
they would assume is forbidden (splitting up, asking strangers, using a phone, leaving the
route), say explicitly that it is allowed. Permission is not a hint, it removes a false barrier.
**Detect:** list what the best team would do. For each: would an ordinary team assume it is
not allowed? Then add one permission sentence. Does the text describe the strategy itself? Then
cut it.
**Caught by:** writing.md "permissions", hard gate G4.
