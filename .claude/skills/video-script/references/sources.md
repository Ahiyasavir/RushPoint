# Where the outside claims come from

Only claims that survived checking are here. Anything not listed was dropped.

**Hook timing.** Viewers decide in roughly the first 1 to 3 seconds; land the hook there,
one short sentence, open a loop, and match it to an arresting first frame.
- https://kineclip.com/blog/how-to-write-viral-hooks-short-form-2026/
- https://virvid.ai/blog/first-3-seconds-hook-faceless-shorts-2026
- https://fobetmedia.com/instagram-reel-hooks/

**Hook, body, close, and layered re hooks.** The hook drives 3s retention, the body drives mid
video retention and rewatches, the close drives saves and shares. Relying on a single opening
hook is no longer enough; hook again through the body.
- https://www.octupie.com/blog/how-to-structure-video-scripts
- https://hypenest.ai/blogs/tiktok-algorithm-2026-video-hooks-retention
- https://isonicmedia.in/beyond-the-first-3-seconds-scripting-reels-hooks-for-2026/

**Founder content.** Lead with a problem, not a product; audiences lean toward what looks
honest over what looks produced; doubter then proof, with the proof a number or a screenshot;
one clip a day, repurposed, is the sustainable solo cadence.
- https://www.socialync.io/blog/founder-build-in-public-content-playbook-2026
- https://www.under30ceo.com/short-form-video-founder-content-2026/

**AI tells.** The tell is structure and rhythm, not vocabulary: stating the lesson, naming
nothing real, tying every bow, forced triads, and uniform sentence length. Fix by varying
sentence length hard and adding a detail only you would know.
- https://aipromptindex.io/guides/remove-ai-slop-from-writing/
- https://www.georgekao.com/blog/118515-how-to-write-without-sounding-like-ai
- https://metric37.com/blog/common-ai-words-and-phrases

**Visual hooks and the first frame.** Feeds reward pattern interruption; the opening stacks a
visual change, a text overlay and a spoken line landing together. The NERV audit (Novelty,
Emotion, Relevance, Void, replace under 12 of 20) comes from pixelplot, **which presents no
data behind it.** Use it as a heuristic, not as evidence.
- https://pixelplot.ai/video-hook-psychology/
- https://www.moonb.io/blog/how-to-create-video-hooks

**Cadence and distribution.** Steady publishing over 20+ weeks beats bursts; three to five a
week is the sustainable band and three well made beats daily low effort; distribution keys on
watch time, saves and comment velocity; short plus long form grows subscribers about 3x faster
than a single format. The percentage claims on those pages (67% faster, 234% more
recommendations) are **uncited vendor numbers, do not repeat them**; the direction is well
supported, the magnitudes are not.
- https://www.teleprompter.com/blog/short-form-video-strategy
- https://chatbotx.io/blog/how-to-grow-on-social-media-in-2026-the-creators-strategy-that-actually-works/
- https://www.shortsync.app/guides/grow-youtube-shorts

**The hook taxonomy.** 457 named patterns across 1,052 teardowns, collapsed to ten families in
`hook-bank.md`. The repo's per pattern view statistics are **not usable**: 472 distinct labels
over 526 videos, so most medians rest on n=3.
- https://github.com/shixinzhang/tiktok-viral-hooks

**Killed, do not cite.** From the earlier research pass on `docs/marketing/playbooks/video-plan.md`:
17 of 25 stat claims failed adversarial checking, including the widely repeated "87% skip in
3 seconds" and "340% engagement". Do not reintroduce them.

**Not found.** No credible Hebrew language source on short form scripting exists beyond agency
marketing pages. The Hebrew half of this skill rests on his own corpus, which is better
evidence anyway.
