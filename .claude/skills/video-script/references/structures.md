# Structures. Pick one by name before writing.

Each one is a proven shape. Do not invent a new shape. Do not blend two.
Research basis is in `sources.md`; the short version is that a short form video is a hook,
a body and a close, the hook decides the three second retention, the body decides mid video
retention, and the close decides saves and shares.

---

## 1. THE INCIDENT  (his default, 30 to 45s)

The shape of the "four in the morning" video. One thing happened. He tells it in order.

```
HOOK      the incident, stated as fact, with its number and its hour
STAKE     why it mattered, one line
ACTIONS   three or four verb stacked beats, in order, each a different shot
TURN      what he found or what broke
CLOSE     what he is doing next, then the invitation
```

Real example, his own words: `עשרות אנשים רצו אצלנו בחמישי האחרון` → `וב4 בבוקר כבר התעוררתי` →
`עשיתי תחקיר` → `סיווגתי כל תקלה` → `הרצתי סימולציות, סגרתי באגים` → `השמש עוד לא עלתה וכבר
התחלתי לבנות את הבאים` → `ואם בא לכם לראות, תעקבו`.

**Use when** something specific happened this week. **This is the default. Reach for it first.**

## 2. THE PHONE CALL  (25 to 40s)

An external party validates him. The devlog 5 shape.

```
HOOK      the setup with two possible readings, unresolved
          כשמנכ״ל של חברה מתקשר אליך באמצע היום, יש רק שתי אפשרויות
BEAT      option A, bad
BEAT      option B, huge
REVEAL    which one it was, named, with the real name
DEFLATE   one honest line that undercuts the glamour
CLOSE     what it changes tomorrow
```

The DEFLATE beat is what stops it reading as a brag. `אני עדיין לא לגמרי מעכל את זה`.

**Use for** Dreams, Adidas, a school, a movement, a new partner, press.

---

## 3. THE NUMBER THAT DOES NOT ADD UP  (20 to 30s)

```
HOOK      two facts that should not both be true
          אני בן 17. בשבוע שעבר מאות בני נוער רצו על האפליקציה שלי
BODY      how, in three concrete beats, no explaining
CLOSE     the next impossible number
```

**Use for** cold audiences and collabs. It is the format that travels furthest, because the
tension is legible in two seconds with no context.

---

## 4. THE THING THAT BROKE  (30 to 45s)

His highest trust format and the least used. Every devlog has a `מה עדיין לא עובד` block.

```
HOOK      the failure, out loud, first
          במרוץ הראשון שלי הסרטונים פשוט לא נפתחו לאף אחד
COST      who it hurt, concretely
DIG       what the real cause turned out to be, one sentence, plain
FIX       what he shipped
CLOSE     what he still does not know
```

**Never end this one resolved.** The unresolved close is the whole point and it is also the
thing an AI draft will not let you keep.

---

## 5. THE FIELD CUT  (15 to 25s, mostly no talking)

Real gameplay footage. Kids running, phones, a finish, a leaderboard flipping.

```
HOOK      3s of the loudest real moment, no caption yet
CAPTION   one line naming what you are seeing
RUN       6 to 12 fast cuts on the beat, real audio kept up
PAYOFF    a face at the finish, or the board flipping
CLOSE     one card, one line, one CTA
```

**The only format that works muted.** Every caption must carry the whole meaning alone.
**Use for** anything aimed at parents, movement leaders and event organisers.

---

## 6. THE ANSWER  (20 to 30s)

Someone asked a real question. He answers it to camera. Nothing else.

```
HOOK      the question, quoted, on screen and spoken
ANSWER    the direct answer in one sentence, immediately
PROOF     one screenshot, one number, one clip
CLOSE     invite the next question
```

**Use when** he has no incident to report. It is the format that always has fuel.

---

## Re hook at 7 seconds

Whatever the structure, the beat landing around second 7 must reopen the loop: a new number,
a cut to a different location, a contradiction. The single hook era is over; long enough
retention now comes from layered hooks through the body, not from one opening line.

## Length by platform

| Slot | Words of Hebrew | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 15s | ~38 | field cut, one idea |
| 30s | ~72 | the default |
| 45s | ~110 | incident with a turn |
| 60s | ~145 | only with real footage carrying it |

Hebrew speech in short form runs about 2.2 to 2.8 words per second. If the script runs long,
cut a beat. Never speed the delivery up.

## 7. THE OPEN INVITATION  (28 to 35s)

Added 2026-09-18, written for the Jerusalem race recruitment post. **Use it whenever the job
is to get strangers to sign up for a real event**, which none of the other six do: they all
assume the viewer already knows what the thing is.

```
CONFESSION   an apology for the information gap, spoken to everyone, no context
             אני מצטער. · אני מצטער שלא סיפרתי לכם על זה קודם.
REVEAL       what it actually is, in ONE sentence a total stranger understands
SCOPE        who it is open to, named concretely, so nobody excludes themselves
SCARCITY     the number, and how much of it is already gone
CTA A        the registration link, alone on screen, given its own beat
CTA B        a tag prompt that makes the comment section do recruitment work
```

**Why the apology opener.** It is the confession family, which `hook-bank.md` ranks second for
him and which he almost never uses. It does three jobs in two words: it is an unresolved
emotional fragment, it acknowledges the viewer knew nothing without making them feel stupid,
and it converts a cold audience's ignorance into an opportunity he is handing them. The
highest performing confession in the teardown sample, 14M views, opens on exactly this.

**Why SCOPE is its own beat.** A stranger's default assumption is that the event is not for
them. Naming four kinds of group out loud (`חברים, משפחה, תנועה, כיתה`) removes that faster
than any adjective. Four, not three, so it does not read as a triad.

**Why two CTAs and in this order.** Registration is the goal, so the link gets a clean beat
with nothing competing. The tag prompt goes last because comments are the distribution lever
(`growth.md`), and because a tag prompt aimed at "who should lead a team" turns the comment
section into influencer outreach the creator can reply to individually.

**The trap:** SCARCITY must be a real number. An invented "only 3 left" is the one line in
this structure that can cost trust permanently, because the audience finds out at the event.
