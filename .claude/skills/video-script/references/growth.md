# Pulling an audience

Scripts are one input. This file is the rest of it, ordered by what the evidence says
actually moves.

---

## The one finding that reorders everything else

> Creators overestimate what a single viral post does and underestimate what a reliable
> posting rhythm compounds into over six months. Steady publishing across 20+ weeks
> dramatically outperforms posting in bursts.

So the goal is not a viral video. **The goal is a rhythm he can hold while in twelfth grade,
running a company and producing a race.** A brilliant script he posts once is worth less than
a good script he posts every Tuesday for five months.

**Realistic cadence for him: three a week.** Three well made beats daily low effort. Five is
the ceiling and he does not have five in him alongside matriculation. Below two it stops
compounding.

The sustainable engine is the one he already runs: **one clip a day of real work, most of them
never posted, three of them cut and shipped.** He is already filming his life for the devlog.

---

## What distribution actually keys on

Watch time, saves and **comment velocity**, not posting frequency alone. That changes how a
script ends.

- **A script that ends on a question outperforms one that ends on a CTA**, because comments
  are the lever. He already writes this way: `איך אתם מפנים זמן לפרויקט צד כשאין זמן? כתבו
  לי, אני קורא הכול.` That closer belongs on the videos too, not only the blog.
- **Leave one deliberate gap.** The highest performing confession in the teardown sample works
  entirely because it leaves the reason unsaid and the comments fill it in. Do not resolve
  everything. This is the same rule as "never tie every bow", arriving from the other side.
- **Saves come from utility.** The one genuinely save worthy thing he owns is *how to run a
  field game for a group without it falling apart*. That is a video for madrichim and parents,
  and it is the only format of his that people bookmark.

## The series is the asset, not the video

Content that rewards a returning viewer is what converts a view into a follow. He already has
the series and has not named it: **the build log.** Five written devlogs exist. The video
version should be numbered on screen, same frame, same opening beat, so an arriving viewer can
see it is episode eleven of something.

**Three series that fit him, in order of strength:**

1. **`יומן בנייה`** — one real day, one real problem. THE INCIDENT structure. Infinite fuel,
   because it is just his life. This is the spine.
2. **`מה נשבר היום`** — the confession family as a running format. Nobody in Hebrew tech
   content does this honestly, it is the clearest available differentiator, and it is the
   format with the best trust per minute.
3. **`בונים משחק ל...`** — a game built live for a named occasion. Utility, saves, and it
   doubles as product marketing without being a demo.

The counting matters. `יום 47` on screen is itself a hook: it implies 46 you missed.

## Where his audiences actually are

**Teens, via @israeli_teens.** 44.7K, verified, agreed to an organic post, still owed.
Measured over 11 posts: median about 1,050 likes, top posts 13.5K to 16K, so distribution is
explore and reels rather than followers. What wins there is authentic raw footage they merely
frame. What loses is their own branded productions, floor of the sample at **148 likes**.

> **A product demo is the exact format that fails on that page.** The brief is a teen did the
> impossible story with real gameplay footage. Organic, unpaid, so it must not read as an ad.
> He wants `אני אחיה` said out loud and one line naming **דרימז**.

Their own caption formula, observed: hook line → first person plural body → a question to the
audience → tag CTA → follow line → single hashtag `#הנוערשלישראל`. Match it.

**Madrichim and movement staff.** The highest intent audience he has and the least worked. He
is one of them, which is an unfair advantage. They share into staff groups, which is
distribution money cannot buy.

**Parents, via birthdays.** Already his live channel. Every birthday event he runs is a room
of parents watching their children have a good time, which is the highest converting
demonstration that exists. **Film those.** One clip per event, permission first.

**Creators and founders.** Smallest, but they are who amplifies a build log.

## The field is his channel, and that is a real strategy

He already decided this and it is correct:

> במקום להשקיע יותר מדי בפרסומים באינסטגרם ובפייסבוק, לקחתי אותה ישר לשטח.

Every run is a shoot. Every birthday is footage. The Jerusalem race in October is the single
biggest content event on his calendar and it should be planned as a shoot as seriously as it
is planned as a race: someone assigned to film, consent handled in advance, and a shot list
built from `visual-hooks.md` Tier 3, which is the tier he is missing entirely.

---

## Cross posting

Same vertical master to Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Do not re edit per platform; it is not worth
his hours. Two things do transfer and are worth the minute:

- Re upload the file rather than sharing a link, so each platform ranks it natively.
- Rewrite the caption per platform. The hook line stays, the CTA changes.

Long form is the one real multiplier he is not using: creators posting both short and long
form grow subscribers roughly 3x faster than single format creators. The cheap version is a
monthly 8 minute cut of that month's clips, zero new filming.

---

## Honest ranking of effort to return

1. **Hold three a week for twenty weeks.** Nothing else on this list competes.
2. **Get Tier 3 footage.** Faces at a finish line. It unlocks every emotional hook.
3. **Name and number the series.**
4. **End on a question, reply to every comment for the first hour.**
5. **Ship the @israeli_teens video.** One time spike, real, already agreed, still owed.
6. **Film the birthdays.**
7. Everything else.

**What to ignore:** posting time optimisation, hashtag research, trending audio chasing, and
follower count as a metric. Timing and format amplify the two levers he controls, showing up
regularly and building real conversation. They do not replace them.
