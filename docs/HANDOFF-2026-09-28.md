## 7. Traps hit (so you do not repeat them)

- `scripts/lib/callableHardening.mjs` attributes a staff capability to a callable by TEXT REGION up to
  the next callable, so a new staff callable must sit where its `assertStaffCan` is inside its own
  region (the flash callables were moved before `pushFlashMission` for this reason).
- A new staff callable must be added to `STAFF_CAPABILITY_BY_CALLABLE` AND to the expected table in
  `scripts/test-staff-capabilities.ts`.
- A new field returned to the phone must be added to the e2e allowlists (`ALLOWED_TASK_KEYS`,
  `ALLOWED_RUN_TEAM_ROW_KEYS`), or e2e fails loud (by design).
- Hebrew copy may not contain `-` or `—` (`test-no-dashes.ts`): write "לחמש דקות", not "ל-5 דקות".
- White text on `bg-danger` fails the a11y contrast scan; use `bg-ink-alert`.
- A clickable `<div>` fails the a11y scan; use a real `<button>` (e.g. a full-backdrop button).
- Editing files with Python: keep the file's own line endings (`newline=''`), check against HEAD.
- The browser pane's screenshots sometimes time out when the window is not drawn; use
  `javascript_tool` / `read_page` for proof instead.
