# Draft missions — the missing middle of the relatedness ladder

**Status: DRAFTS. Not in `TASK_BANK`.** Written for review first, per the standing
preference that mission candidates get looked at before they become code.

## Why these two

Rule 61 audited the bank's disclosure ladder by position and found a bottom rung
(the openers: a word each, a name and a movement each — turn-taking with zero
disclosure, correct for minute zero), a top rung (`finish-what-we-didnt-know`,
which is Aron's third set almost word for word), and **nothing in between**. The
closing mission therefore lands on a group being asked to be sincere on command
rather than on one that has been warming up for an hour.

Rule 66 then rules out the obvious fix. Writing "say one thing about another
person" at two depths makes them one `family`, and the composer places exactly
one of them — the ladder collapses to a single step. The rungs have to escalate
through **different verbs**.

The four-verb ladder, with what exists and what is missing:

| depth | verb | mission | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contribute a token | openers (a word, a name + a movement) | exists |
| 2 | **state a fact about yourself** | — | **draft A** |
| 3 | give an opinion about today | `best-moment-so-far` (now spoken aloud) | exists |
| 4 | **predict something about a teammate** | — | **draft B** |
| 5 | observe something about a teammate | `finish-what-we-didnt-know` | exists |

Both drafts are also deliberately **not photo missions**. Rule 54 measured the
bank at 61 of 103 photo submissions (59%), which makes every composed game's
middle self-similar and leaves it with no peak; `survey` and `self_report` are at
two entries each.

## Draft A — rung 2 · "הכי רחוק שהיינו"

**Type** `survey` (free text) · **difficulty** 2 (`easy`) · **transit** 0 ·
**tags** `thinking`, `teamwork`, `noPrep`, `fromAnywhere`, every area + `indoor`
+ `outdoor`, `kids`, `youth`, `adults`, `corporate`, `mixed`, `easy`

> כל אחד בתורו אומר את המקום הכי רחוק מכאן שהוא היה בו אי פעם. אחרי שכולם אמרו,
> סדרו את עצמכם בשורה לפי המרחק, מהקרוב לרחוק. כתבו כאן את המקום הרחוק ביותר
> שיצא לכם.

**Against the rules**

- **62 (structural reciprocity)** — "כל אחד בתורו" and everyone appears in the
  ordering. It cannot be completed by the loudest member.
- **63 (depth ↔ minute)** — a factual, non-evaluative disclosure. Nobody is asked
  to be vulnerable; they are asked where they have been. Safe at minute twenty.
- **63 (a group is not a pair)** — one sentence, sayable standing in a circle.
- **52** — autonomy weak, competence yes (the ordering is a real task with a
  right answer), relatedness yes. 2/3, which is where most good missions sit.
- **60 (self-checkable)** — the line is either in the right order or it is not.
- **64 (named target)** — "order yourselves by distance" is specific; there is no
  "share something interesting" anywhere in it.
- **54** — a `survey`, not a photo.

**Open question for review:** should it be `survey` (captures the answer, useful
for a recap screen) or `self_report` (cheaper, no data)? Survey seems better —
the furthest place is a nice thing to surface at the finish.

## Draft B — rung 4 · "ניחוש על החבר"

**Type** `self_report` · **difficulty** 4 (`medium`) · **transit** 0 ·
**tags** `thinking`, `teamwork`, `noPrep`, `fromAnywhere`, every area + `indoor`
+ `outdoor`, `youth`, `adults`, `corporate`, `mixed`, `medium`

> אחד מכם עומד בצד ושותק. השאר מנחשים עליו חמישה דברים בקול: מה הוא אוכל בבוקר,
> לאן היה נוסע מחר אם היה יכול, מה הוא עושה כשמשעמם לו, איזה מקצוע היה בוחר, ומה
> הוא הכי לא סובל. אחרי כל ניחוש הוא אומר רק "קלעתם" או "לא". המטרה: שלושה מתוך
> חמישה. עברו לבא בתור, עד שכולם היו.

**Against the rules**

- **66 (different verb)** — the mechanic is *predict and confirm*, not "say
  something about". No family collision with the finisher.
- **62** — "עברו לבא בתור, עד שכולם היו" makes every member both subject and
  guesser. Reciprocity is written in, not hoped for.
- **60 (feedback)** — "קלעתם / לא" after every guess. This is one of the few
  missions in the bank with genuine immediate feedback, which is flow's second
  condition and the thing 61 auto-approved photo missions cannot provide.
- **64 (specific difficult goal)** — "three out of five", not "get to know each
  other". A target that directs effort and prompts strategy (they start
  reasoning about the person rather than guessing at random).
- **63** — the five prompts are deliberately mundane, and the subject only ever
  says one word, so nobody is required to explain themselves. Mid-game safe.
- **51** — entirely within the team, no public exposure.
- **52** — autonomy (which guesses to make), competence (the 3/5 target),
  relatedness (the whole point). 3/3.
- **50** — "אחד מכם" is a role by function and rotates; no headcount assumed.

**Open questions for review:**

1. Five prompts fixed, or should the creator be able to edit them via Quick Setup?
   Fixed is simpler and keeps `noPrep` honest (rule 30).
2. Time: with five people this is five rounds. Estimated 8 minutes — is that too
   long for a mid-game mission?
3. `kids` is deliberately absent. "מה הוא הכי לא סובל" and "איזה מקצוע היה בוחר"
   both want a settled self-concept. Should there be a `kids` variant with
   simpler prompts, or is that a family collision with itself?

---

## Not drafted, and why

A third rung between 2 and 3 was considered and dropped: it would have been
another "state a fact" mission, which rule 66 says the composer would never place
alongside draft A. The ladder is better with four well-spaced rungs than five
that fight each other.
