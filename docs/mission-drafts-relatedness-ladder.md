# Draft missions — the missing middle of the relatedness ladder

**Status: DRAFTS. Not in `TASK_BANK`.** Written for review first, per the standing
preference that mission candidates get looked at before they become code.

**Revised 2026-09-02 (v2).** Both drafts were written before the pre-flight
checklist existed. Running the nine items against them found a real defect in
each — see *What the re-audit caught* below. The versions here are the fixed ones.

## Why these two

Rule 61 audited the bank's disclosure ladder by position and found a bottom rung
(the openers: a word each, a name and a movement each — turn-taking with zero
disclosure, correct for minute zero), a top rung (`finish-what-we-didnt-know`,
which is Aron's third set almost word for word), and **nothing in between**. The
closing mission therefore lands on a group being asked to be sincere on command
rather than on one that has been warming up for an hour.

Rule 66 then rules out the obvious fix. Writing "say one thing about another
person" at two depths makes them one `family`, and the composer places exactly
one of them. The rungs have to escalate through **different verbs**.

| depth | verb | mission | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | contribute a token | openers (a word, a name + a movement) | exists |
| 2 | **state a fact about yourself** | — | **draft A** |
| 3 | give an opinion about today | `best-moment-so-far` (now spoken aloud) | exists |
| 4 | **predict something about a teammate** | — | **draft B** |
| 5 | observe something about a teammate | `finish-what-we-didnt-know` | exists |

Both are deliberately **not photo missions**. Rule 54 measured the bank at 61 of
103 photo submissions (59%); `survey` and `self_report` sit at two each.

## Draft A — rung 2 · "הכי רחוק שהיינו"

**Type** `survey` (free text) · **difficulty** 2 (`easy`) · **transit** 0 ·
**tags** `thinking`, `teamwork`, `noPrep`, `fromAnywhere`, every area + `indoor`
+ `outdoor`, `kids`, `youth`, `adults`, `corporate`, `mixed`, `easy`

> כל אחד בתורו אומר את המקום הכי רחוק מכאן שהוא היה בו אי פעם, וכמה זמן ארכה
> הדרך לשם. אחרי שכולם אמרו, סדרו את עצמכם בשורה לפי אורך הנסיעה, מהקצרה לארוכה.
> כתבו כאן את המקום שיצא רחוק ביותר.

**Changed in v2 — rule 56.** The first version asked the group to order itself by
DISTANCE. That is a geographic comparison — is Athens further than Eilat? — and a
nine-year-old does not reliably hold it, which makes the mission's central
operation one its `kids` tag promises it does not demand. Ordering by **how long
the journey took** is the same mission with a concrete, experienced quantity
underneath it: a child knows they flew four hours or drove one. Rule 57's second
route, arriving at the design stage instead of as a repair.

**Against the rules**

- **62** — "כל אחד בתורו", and everyone appears in the ordering.
- **63** — factual and non-evaluative. Nobody is asked to be vulnerable; they are
  asked where they have been. Safe at minute twenty.
- **60** — the line is either in the right order or it is not, and the group can
  check it against the times they just said out loud.
- **64** — "order yourselves by journey length" is a named target, not "share
  something interesting about yourself".
- **52** — autonomy weak, competence yes (the ordering is a real task), relatedness
  yes. 2/3, where most good missions sit.
- **54** — a `survey`, not a photo.

## Draft B — rung 4 · "ניחוש על החבר"

**Type** `self_report` · **difficulty** 4 (`medium`) · **transit** 0 ·
**tags** `thinking`, `teamwork`, `noPrep`, `fromAnywhere`, every area + `indoor`
+ `outdoor`, `youth`, `adults`, `corporate`, `mixed`, `medium`

> אחד מכם עומד בצד ושותק, וכל אחד מהשאר לוקח שאלה אחת ומנחש עליו בקול: מה הוא
> אוכל בבוקר, לאן היה נוסע מחר אם היה יכול, מה הוא עושה כשמשעמם לו, איזה מקצוע
> היה בוחר, ומה הוא הכי לא סובל. אחרי כל ניחוש הוא אומר רק "קלעתם" או "לא".
> עברו לבא בתור, עד שכולם היו. המטרה של כולכם ביחד: עשרה ניחושים נכונים לאורך כל
> הסבבים.

**Changed in v2 — two defects, both caught by the pre-flight.**

**Item 2 (could one player do this while the others watch?) — rule 68.** The
first version said "השאר מנחשים", which hands the whole mission to whoever talks
fastest while the rest stand there. Now **each guesser takes one of the five
questions**, so every offer is load-bearing and the mission scales with team size
instead of assuming one.

**Item 6 (if there is a target, whose is it?) — rule 69.** The first version set
"three out of five" **per subject**. That is a public score on one named person's
round, and a round that comes out 0/5 is a group announcing that it does not know
them — which for a fourteen-year-old is exactly the social-evaluative threat rule
69 exists to keep out of this bank. The target is now **cumulative across the
whole rotation**: no individual round is scored, and the number belongs to
everybody. Same difficulty, same feedback, no one left standing under it.

**Against the rules**

- **66** — the mechanic is *predict and confirm*, not "say something about". No
  family collision with the finisher.
- **62** — "עברו לבא בתור, עד שכולם היו": every member is both subject and guesser.
- **60** — "קלעתם / לא" after every guess. One of the few missions in this bank
  with genuine immediate feedback, which is flow's second condition.
- **64** — "ten correct across all the rounds" is a specific, difficult, named
  target that prompts strategy rather than exhorting effort.
- **63** — the five prompts are deliberately mundane and the subject only ever
  says one word, so nobody has to explain themselves.
- **51 / 71** — entirely inside the team. No public exposure, no strangers.
- **52** — autonomy (which guess to make), competence (the cumulative target),
  relatedness (the whole point). 3/3.
- **50** — "אחד מכם" is a role by function and it rotates; no headcount assumed.

**Open questions for review**

1. Five prompts fixed, or creator-editable via Quick Setup? Fixed keeps `noPrep`
   honest (rule 30) and stops a creator writing a prompt that lands badly.
2. Five people means five rounds. Estimated 8 minutes — too long for mid-game?
   Rule 75 says price it against neighbours: `two-truths-one-lie` is 8 minutes,
   which is the closest thing here.
3. `kids` is deliberately absent. "מה הוא הכי לא סובל" and "איזה מקצוע היה בוחר"
   both want a settled self-concept (rule 56). Rule 73 says the answer is not a
   diluted version but a different mission for that audience — so a kids rung 4
   should be drafted separately, if it is wanted at all.

## What the re-audit caught

Worth recording because it is evidence about the checklist rather than about
these two missions. Both drafts were written on the night rule 66 was added, by
the same process that had just finished arguing for them, and both looked
finished. Two nights later the pre-flight existed, and running its nine items
over them found one defect each — item 2 and item 6, both in draft B, both
invisible to the person who had written it and obvious the moment the question
was asked in order.

That is the case for DO-CONFIRM (rule 74's format choice) stated in evidence: the
author is the last person able to see what their own draft is missing, and a
short fixed list asked afterwards is what closes the gap.
